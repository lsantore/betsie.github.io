How easy is Betsie Quick Bet?

  1. 1 Create

    Add your names, the bet, end date, and a little trash talk.

    Send the link in iMessage, SMS, or WhatsApp.

  2. 2 Challenge

    Your friend agrees or picks the other side, then sends it back.

    The countdown starts. Add it to your calendar.

  3. 3 Settle

    Vote the outcome when time is up, or call it early.

    Get a Bet Receipt and share it in chat or on social.

Create, Bet, Repeat. Keep the score and keep it fun.
