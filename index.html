How it works

Four short steps from idea to receipt—transparent rules, no surprises.

Set the scene

Choose a prediction, invite someone, and set a timeframe. They accept before the clock starts—clear terms, no retro edits.

Optional stakes

Add a fun, low-stakes consequence if you want: coffee, a silly hat, a story post. It is always optional and between you two.

Close it together

When the moment arrives, both sides confirm the outcome in the app. If you disagree, you work it out—nothing posts without alignment.

Memorialize the record

Wins and losses update your profile stats—so the group chat can move on, but the receipts stay crisp.
