Betsie – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Squeaky Cheese, Inc. ("Betsie", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Betsie app and related services (the "App"). Betsie is a social app for friendly predictions. Bets on Betsie involve no real money.

This policy works together with our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as:

  • Account details: your name, email address, username, profile photo, and phone number when you create an account. We require a phone number for every new account.
  • Sign-in details: if you sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook, we receive the account details those services share with us, such as your name, email address, and a sign-in identifier. Signing in with one of those services does not replace the phone number. You still provide a phone number to create the account.
  • Contacts: if you use find friends, we access the contacts you choose to share so we can help you find people you know on Betsie. We do not use your contacts unless you use that feature.
  • Content: bets, predictions, outcomes, comments, chats, images, and any other content you create or send in the App.
  • Communications: messages you send to other users or to us directly.

We also collect information automatically when you use the App, including:

  • Usage data: features you use, time spent in the App, and other interaction data.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
  • Location: general location derived from your IP address.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's services and features, including your account, the content you post, find friends, sign-in, notifications, email, and GIFs.
  • Personalize your experience, such as showing relevant bets or friends.
  • Communicate with you about updates, security alerts, and support messages.
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities in connection with the App.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or illegal activity, apply our rules, and review reports.

3. Advertising and analytics

Betsie is ad-supported. We use third-party advertising partners and analytics providers to help us run, measure, and improve the App. We do not allow advertisers access to:

  • Bet content
  • Personal messages
  • Sensitive user attributes

4. Who else handles your information

These companies process information so they can provide the services below. We do not sell your personal information.

  • Amazon hosts Betsie and sends email.
  • Google Firebase delivers notifications.
  • Google, Apple, and Facebook provide sign-in, if you choose to use them. Their own privacy policies apply to your account with them.
  • Klipy provides GIFs. If you search for or send a GIF, that request is handled by Klipy.

We may also share limited information with:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics providers
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Content moderation tools
  • Legal authorities when necessary

We do not share bets, predictions, or sensitive user content with advertisers.

5. Reporting and blocking

You can report content and block people in the App. Blocking limits that person's ability to interact with you. When you report content, we review it and may remove the content or the account that posted it.

6. How long we keep information

We retain your data as long as your account remains active.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the App, or on the web at origin.betsie.app/account/delete.

Deletion starts a 14-day grace period. During those 14 days you can sign back in and cancel. When the 14 days end, we delete your account and the personal information associated with it. Content you already sent to other people, such as a chat message, may remain in their conversation.

8. Your rights

You may:

  • Access or correct your data
  • Request deletion
  • Opt out of personalized ads where legally required
  • Request details on our data processing

9. Age

Betsie is for adults 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

10. No real money

Bets on Betsie are for entertainment. They do not involve real money, cash prizes, or anything of monetary value.

11. Changes

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. That date is the version of this policy in effect.

12. Contact

Write to privacy@betsie.app with questions about your data or this Privacy Policy. You can also reach us from our Support page.
